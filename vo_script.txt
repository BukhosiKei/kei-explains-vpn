What is a VPN?

Every time you go online,
your data travels across a public road.

Anyone along the way
could look at what you're doing.

A VPN creates a private tunnel
between your device and the internet.

Your data travels through it, scrambled —
unreadable to anyone outside.

The website sees the VPN,
not your real address.

In short:
a VPN is a private tunnel for your data.
